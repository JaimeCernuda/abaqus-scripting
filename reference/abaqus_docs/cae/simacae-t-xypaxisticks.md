The number of major and minor tick marks is controlled separately; see [Customizing the X–Y plot axis tick mode](simacae-t-xypaxistickmode.md). You can control the X- and Y-axis tick marks independently. Examples of major and minor tick marks are shown in [Figure 1](simacae-t-xypaxisticks.md#simacae-t-xypaxisticks__simacae-t-viw-tickmarks). Figure 1. X–Y plot with major and minor tick marks indicated.     Major tick marks govern the placement of the X–Y plot major [grid](simacae-t-xypchartoptionsgrid.md) and the [numeric axis labels](simacae-t-xypaxislabels.md). Minor tick marks are shorter, unlabeled marks governing the placement of the minor gridlines.  By default, Abaqus/CAE automatically computes the number of tick marks. You can specify major tick marks by giving the increment between them or the total number of tick marks. You can specify minor tick marks only by giving the total number between each major tick mark.  The color of the tick marks is governed by the color of the axes. For more information, see [Customizing the X–Y plot axis color and style](simacae-t-xypaxisstyle.md).  1. Locate the axis Tick Marks options.
