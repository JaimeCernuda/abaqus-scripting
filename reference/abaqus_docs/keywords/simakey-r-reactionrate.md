This option is used to define the reaction rate for an ignition and growth equation of state. It is required when the [EOS](simakey-r-eos.md#simakey-r-eos), TYPE=IGNITION AND GROWTH option is used. The [REACTION RATE](simakey-r-reactionrate.md#simakey-r-reactionrate) option should appear immediately after the [EOS](simakey-r-eos.md#simakey-r-eos) or the [GAS SPECIFIC HEAT](simakey-r-gasspecificheat.md#simakey-r-gasspecificheat) option.
