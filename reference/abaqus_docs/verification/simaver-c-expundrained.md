Problem description Abaqus uses a unit cube of geometry with distorted meshes to test three-dimensional solid elements and a simple rectangular geometry to test plane strain elements as well as axisymmetric solid elements. The skeleton materials are linear isotropic elastic with    E = 3000 ,    ρ = 1200 , and    ν = 0.3 . The permeating pore fluid has a density,      ρ  w = 1000 , and a bulk modulus,      K  w = 2.1   e  9 . The bulk modulus of the grains is      K  g = 3.7   e  7 . The medium is subject to prescribed displacement boundary conditions to control volumetric deformation. You can compare the numerical results with a reference solution.  Reference solution The reference solution is based on the analytical equations of a fully saturated medium with prescribed volumetric deformation under undrained conditions. This leads to a nonlinear system of equations that is solved numerically.  Results and discussion All tests using different element types produce the expected results, and the pore pressure agrees well with the reference solution.
