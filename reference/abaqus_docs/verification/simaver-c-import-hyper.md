A slightly compressible hyperelastic material is used in the analyses. The sequence of tests involves transferring results from Abaqus/Standard to Abaqus/Explicit and then back into Abaqus/Standard.  Verification tests are also included for some first-order reduced-integration elements with enhanced hourglass control.  Four types of hyperelastic strain energy potentials are considered: the polynomial, Ogden, Arruda-Boyce, and van der Waals forms.
