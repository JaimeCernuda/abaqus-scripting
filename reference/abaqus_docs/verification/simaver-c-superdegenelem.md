Problem description A planar substructure is formed from standard CPS8 elements and CPS8 elements degenerated into 6-node triangles. Both displacement degrees of freedom are retained at selected nodes on the substructure that is constrained as depicted below, and a displacement of 0.2 in the x-direction is prescribed for the three nodes along x=1.0.  Results and discussion The results are identical to the analytical results where a stress of 6 × 106 develops in the direction of extension.
