This page discusses:   * [Elements tested](#simaver-c-expansion-elem) * [Problem description](#simaver-c-expansion-desc) * [Results and discussion](#simaver-c-expansion-result) * [Input files](#simaver-c-expansion-input) | | |   Products Abaqus/Standard  Elements tested C3D8PT  QEC3D8  Problem description The model consists of a cube of porous material of initial side length      L  0  and volume      V  0 =     L  0  3 , which is subjected to unconstrained thermal and field expansion. The expansion is assumed to be isotropic, and geometric nonlinearity is assumed in all the calculations. Due to stress-free expansion, the overall volume ratio of the porous medium,    J , is equal to the volume ratio of the solid grains,      J  g , and the porosity, n, stays constant during the expansion. Denoting the logarithmic expansion strains in the solid grains and in the pore fluid as      ϵ  s  and      ϵ  f , respectively, the corresponding stretches are      λ  s = exp ⁡  (   ϵ  s )  and      λ  s = exp ⁡  (   ϵ  f ) , so that the expanded cube side length becomes        L  1 = λ  s   L  0 , with volume      V  1 =     L  1  3 . Assuming the porous medium to be fully saturated over the history of the simulation, the initial volume of the pore fluid is      V  0 =     L  0  3 n . The expanded fluid volume is      V  1 f =     L  1 f  3 n , where      L  1 f =     λ  f    L  0 . The change of the total volume for the pores is    Δ   V  p =  (   V  1 −   V  0 ) n , while the change of volume of the pore fluid due to fluid expansion is    Δ   V  f =   V  1 f −   V  0 f . If      ϵ  s  and      ϵ  f  are different from each other, the volume of fluid that must enter the model to maintain full saturation is    Δ   V  p − Δ   V  f . For an 8-node element, the nodal reactive fluid total volume (output variable RVT) is obtained as     (   Δ V  p −   Δ V  f ) (   V  0 f /   V  1 f ) / 8 , where the fluid volume ratio accounts for expansion of the incoming fluid.  The model has three elements with different material properties. The solid and the pore fluid phases in the elements are subjected to a combination of different sources of expansion (multiple field expansions, as well as thermal expansion), such that the total      ϵ  s  and      ϵ  f  are the same in all three elements. Even though the total values of      ϵ  s  and      ϵ  f  are the same for all three elements, the sources of expansion are different in each case. For example, only elements 1 and 3 have the field expansion in the pore fluid due to field variable 3, FVEFL3, defined (see [Figure 2](#simaver-c-expansion-desc__fig_fz1_zn4_qzb); note that element 2 does not show any contour because it does not contain output variable FVEFL3).Figure 2. Field expansion strain due to field variable number 3.  Results and discussion A transient soils consolidation analysis with 10 time increments is carried out. The numerical solutions from Abaqus for the total expansion and RVT agree very well with the analytical solution.
