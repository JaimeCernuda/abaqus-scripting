The simple model geometry and material properties were chosen to clearly depict the motion and orientation of the heat source as it moves through the model. Model  The model is a 10 × 10 × 10 cube.  Mesh  A uniform 20 × 20 × 20 mesh of DC3D8 elements is used.  Material  All cubes have the same constant thermal properties. The low thermal conductivity was chosen to slow the diffusion of heat in the model so that it is easier to see the path of the heat source.   |  |  | | --- | --- | | Conductivity | k  = 5.70496e-3 | | Density | ρ  = 4.43e-09 | | Specific heat | c  p = 543917000 |   (The units are not important.)  Boundary conditions  Each cube is insulated on all faces. An initial temperature of 300° is prescribed for all elements.  Loading  A moving uniform heat source is applied to the model.  Results and discussion In all cases, the heating patterns are as expected. The temperature output variable (NT11) is used to track the thermal pattern.
