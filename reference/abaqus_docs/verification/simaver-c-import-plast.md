The sequence of tests involves transferring results from Abaqus/Explicit to Abaqus/Standard and then back to Abaqus/Explicit. An elastic-plastic material with Mises yield criterion is used in the analyses. Two sets of problems are tested: one with isotropic hardening and the other with combined isotropic/kinematic hardening.
