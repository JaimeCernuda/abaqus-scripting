Related topics:  [Progressive damage and failure in fiber-reinforced materials](simaver-c-damagefrm.md).  2024 FD02 Undrained Pore Fluid Flow and Stress Analysis New tests verify undrained pore fluid flow and stress analysis.  Benefits: The tests verify the capability in Abaqus/Explicit.  A unit cube of geometry with distorted meshes is used to test three-dimensional solid elements, and a simple rectangular geometry is used to test plane strain elements as well as axisymmetric solid elements. The medium is subject to prescribed displacement boundary conditions, and the numerical results are compared with a reference solution.  Related topics:  [Undrained pore fluid flow and stress analysis](simaver-c-expundrained.md).  2024 FD01 Progressive Damage and Failure in Fiber-Reinforced Materials New tests contain basic cases to model progressive damage and failure in fiber-reinforced materials with multiscale modeling. Field Expansion New tests verify the field expansion capability for both solid and pore fluid phases in a porous medium.  Benefits: The tests verify the capability in Abaqus/Standard.  The solid grains and the pore fluid in a porous medium are subjected to thermal expansion and multiple field expansions. Both built-in capabilities and user subroutine [UEXPAN](.._SIMACAESUBRefMap_simasub-c-uexpan.md) are used.
