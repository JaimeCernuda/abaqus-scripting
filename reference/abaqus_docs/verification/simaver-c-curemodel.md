The tests verify the correctness of the degree of cure, the cure shrinkage strain, and the amount of heat generation computed by the model. The tests verify the cure modeling capabilities for both the implementation using special-purpose built-in material options (see [Modeling the Cure Process in Thermosetting Polymers](.._SIMACAEMATRefMap_simamat-c-curemodel.md)) and the implementation using dedicated cure modeling options and output variables (see [Curing Processes in Polymers](.._SIMACAEMATRefMap_simamat-c-curingprocesses.md)).  Most of the tests consist of one element. In those tests, no stresses or thermal loads are applied, and sufficient boundary conditions are prescribed to prevent rigid body motion. In all cases, an initial temperature of 75 is prescribed. To begin the curing process, initial conditions are used to assign small initial values of degree of cure equal to 0.001.  In addition to the one-element tests, this verification includes a curing model of a bilayer strip (see [Figure 1](#exafiguresect_k5g_f2g_nwb__simaver-c-curemodel-bilayer-model)). The model consists of a steel strip coated with an adhesive. The properties of the adhesive material are identical to those used in the one-element tests. The steel is modeled as a linear elastic material with properties given in [Table 11](#simaver-c-curemodel-material__simaver-c-curemodel-steel-properties-bilayer). The model setup is representative of the Kappa Gauge method used to study deformation developed during curing processes due to thermal expansion coefficients mismatch and cure shrinkage (see [Measurement of web curl](https://shareok.org/handle/11244/322065) [[Lindeman, 2021]](#simaver-c-curemodel-bibliography)). For verification purposes, we use the model to verify the correctness of the results at the location indicated in [Figure 1](#exafiguresect_k5g_f2g_nwb__simaver-c-curemodel-bilayer-model).
