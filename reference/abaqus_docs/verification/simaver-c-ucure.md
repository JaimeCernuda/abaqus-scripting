The Kamal and tabular cure kinetics models are implemented in the user subroutine, and the results are compared with the results obtained using equivalent native Abaqus models.  All the tests consist of one element. No stresses or thermal loads are applied, and sufficient boundary conditions are prescribed to prevent rigid body motion. In all cases, an initial temperature of 75 is prescribed. To begin the curing process, initial conditions are used to assign small initial values of degree of cure equal to 0.001.
