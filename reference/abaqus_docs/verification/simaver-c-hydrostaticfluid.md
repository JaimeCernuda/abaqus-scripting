Results and discussion The tests in this section are set up as cases of homogeneous deformation of a “block” of hydrostatic fluid. The fluid pressure, temperature, and cavity volume at the cavity reference node are the results of interest. The pressure reported for the pneumatic fluid is the gauge pressure, not the absolute pressure.  The following five steps are executed:   1. Load 2. Increase fluid temperature 3. Add prescribed amount of fluid 4. Remove prescribed amount of fluid 5.
