Subsequently, shear forces are applied, such that τe⁢q<τc⁢r⁢i⁢t, to verify the “stick” condition. Afterward, prescribed displacements are applied to the rods to force them to slide around the surface.  The contact between the bottom end of the rod and the rigid surface is modeled by specifying a main-secondary contact pair. The bottom end of the rod constitutes the secondary surface created with the node-based surface procedure and has a contact area of unity; hence, the normal force applied on the rod is equal to the contact pressure. Each rod has its separate surface interaction created with the contact property and friction definitions. Further, friction properties are modified during the analysis.
