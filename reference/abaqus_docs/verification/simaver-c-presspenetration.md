Problem description We consider fluid pressure penetration loading for the six model configurations shown in [Figure 1](#simaver-c-presspenetration-t-probdescr1__simaver-c-verpresspen-deform-rigid) through [Figure 6](#simaver-c-presspenetration-t-probdescr1__fig_wvl_twq_pvb). Fluid pressure penetration loading is such that the eventual extent of contact surface regions exposed to fluid pressure are determined during a simulation as contact conditions change. Figure 1. Contact between two deformable cylindrical surfaces in three dimensions.    Figure 6. Contact between two flat deformable plates with a hole in the center of one plate.  Model: Brief descriptions of the model configurations follow:   * [Figure 1](#simaver-c-presspenetration-t-probdescr1__simaver-c-verpresspen-deform-rigid)   shows a two-dimensional model with an interface between a deformable flat surface and a   rigid flat surface. * [Figure 2](#simaver-c-presspenetration-t-probdescr1__fig_a3k_mwq_pvb) shows a   two-dimensional model with an interface between two deformable flat surfaces with   matching meshes. * [Figure 3](#simaver-c-presspenetration-t-probdescr1__fig_u1c_pwq_pvb) shows a   two-dimensional model with an interface between two deformable flat surfaces with   nonmatching meshes. * [Figure 4](#simaver-c-presspenetration-t-probdescr1__fig_v55_qwq_pvb) shows a   three-dimensional model with an interface between deformable and analytical cylindrical   rigid surfaces. * [Figure 5](#simaver-c-presspenetration-t-probdescr1__fig_ukd_swq_pvb) shows a   three-dimensional model with an interface between two deformable cylindrical   surfaces. * [Figure 6](#simaver-c-presspenetration-t-probdescr1__fig_wvl_twq_pvb) shows a   three-dimensional model with an interface between two flat deformable plates with a hole   in the center of one plate.  Material: All deformable bodies in the models have the following properties:  Young's modulus = 1 × 105; Poisson's ratio = 0.3.  Boundary conditions: For the models illustrated in [Figure 1](#simaver-c-presspenetration-t-probdescr1__simaver-c-verpresspen-deform-rigid), [Figure 2](#simaver-c-presspenetration-t-probdescr1__fig_a3k_mwq_pvb), and [Figure 3](#simaver-c-presspenetration-t-probdescr1__fig_u1c_pwq_pvb), the rigid surface is constrained in all degrees of freedom. When the contact between two deformable surfaces is considered, the nodes at    x = ​  0 in [Figure 2](#simaver-c-presspenetration-t-probdescr1__fig_a3k_mwq_pvb) and the nodes at    y = ​  0 in [Figure 3](#simaver-c-presspenetration-t-probdescr1__fig_u1c_pwq_pvb) are constrained in all degrees of freedom.  For the models illustrated in [Figure 4](#simaver-c-presspenetration-t-probdescr1__fig_v55_qwq_pvb) and [Figure 5](#simaver-c-presspenetration-t-probdescr1__fig_ukd_swq_pvb), the rigid surface is constrained in all degrees of freedom. The nodes at the two ends of the rings are constrained in the first degree of freedom, and the nodes at the back surfaces are constrained in the third degree of freedom; when the contact between two deformable surfaces is considered, the inner and outer ring surfaces of the model are also constrained in all degrees of freedom.  For the model illustrated in [Figure 6](#simaver-c-presspenetration-t-probdescr1__fig_wvl_twq_pvb), the nodes on the top of the top plate and the nodes on the sides of both the top and bottom plates are constrained in all degrees of freedom.  Loading: For the models illustrated in [Figure 1](simaver-c-presspenetration.md#simaver-c-presspenetration-t-probdescr1__simaver-c-verpresspen-deform-rigid) and [Figure 2](#simaver-c-presspenetration-t-probdescr1__fig_a3k_mwq_pvb):  Step 1:  A nonuniform displacement,    u =   u 14  1.25 d , is applied in the negative x-direction on the surface of    x = ​  2.0 when solid elements are used.    u 14  is the displacement at node 14, and d is the distance measured from node 14. When shell elements are used, a nonuniform pressure,    P =   P 14 ⁢  (  1 -    d ⁢ c - 0.75 9 ) , is applied on the surface of    x = ​  2.0.    P 14  is the pressure at the element containing node 14, and    d ⁢ c  is the distance measured from node 14 to the center of an element.  Step 2:  Two ends of the contacting surfaces (   y = ​  0 and    y = ​  12.0) are exposed to a fluid pressure with a magnitude of 800.0.  Step 3:  Increase the fluid pressure to 900.0 at both ends.  For the model illustrated in [Figure 3](#simaver-c-presspenetration-t-probdescr1__fig_u1c_pwq_pvb):  Step 1:  A nonuniform displacement,    u =   u 14  1.25 d , is applied in the negative y-direction on the surface of    y = ​  2.0.  Step 2:  One end of the contacting surfaces,    x = ​  2, is exposed to a fluid pressure with a magnitude of 550; and the other end,    x = ​  14, is exposed to a fluid pressure with a magnitude of 800.  Step 3:  Increase the magnitudes of the fluid pressure to 650 from 550 and to 900 from 800, respectively, at both ends.  For the models illustrated in [Figure 4](#simaver-c-presspenetration-t-probdescr1__fig_v55_qwq_pvb) and [Figure 5](#simaver-c-presspenetration-t-probdescr1__fig_ukd_swq_pvb):  Step 1:  The models consist of two parts with contact surfaces initially overclosed. This initial overclosure is resolved automatically in the step by using the automatic shrink fit capability in the contact interference definition.  Step 2:  There are two places initially exposed to a fluid pressure with a magnitude of 20000: at the outside corner of the contact surfaces at the bottom end of the rings and along the whole edges of the contact surfaces at the upper end of the rings.  For the model illustrated in [Figure 6](#simaver-c-presspenetration-t-probdescr1__fig_wvl_twq_pvb):  Step 1:  A nonuniform pressure is applied to the bottom surface of the bottom plate, as shown.  Step 2:  Fluid is initially exposed at the left end of the bottom plate,    x = ​  0 and    y = ​  1, and the left end of the top plate,    x = ​  3 and    y = ​  1. The fluid becomes exposed across the hole before being impeded by the contact between the bottom surface of the top plate and the top surface of the bottom plate.
