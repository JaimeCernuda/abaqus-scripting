Various types of constraints are used that couple the inside surface of the hole to the reference node at the center of the plate. A uniform increase in temperature is prescribed at all nodes including the reference node of the constraint.  Results and discussion A uniform expansion of the plate and uniform expansion of the constraint is observed. No stresses are generated in the plate.
