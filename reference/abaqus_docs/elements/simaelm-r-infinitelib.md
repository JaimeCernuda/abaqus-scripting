Element Property Definition For two-dimensional, plane strain, and plane stress elements, you must provide the thickness of the elements; by default, unit thickness is assumed.  For three-dimensional and axisymmetric solid elements, you do not need to specify a thickness.  For acoustic elements, you must specify the reference point in addition to the thickness. Element Output Stress, Strain, and Other Tensor Components No output is available from Abaqus/Explicit for infinite elements. Stress and other tensors (including strain tensors) are available from Abaqus/Standard for infinite elements with displacement degrees of freedom. All tensors have the same components. For example, the stress components are as follows:  S11  X⁢X direct stress or radial stress for axisymmetric elements.  S22  Y⁢Y direct stress or axial stress for axisymmetric elements.  S33  Z⁢Z direct stress (not available for plane stress elements) or hoop stress for axisymmetric elements.  S12  X⁢Y shear stress or shear stress for axisymmetric elements.  S13  X⁢Z shear stress (not available for plane stress, plane strain, and axisymmetric elements).  S23  Y⁢Z shear stress (not available for plane stress, plane strain, and axisymmetric elements).
