From Hicks we have expressions describing the actual load on the beam in terms of the cross-sectional area of the beam and the response as a function of the beam's structural mass and the entrained fluid mass. Therefore, the acceleration of a rigid mass is  u¨=-μ⁢(r0)⁢∇⁡P⁢1ρf,  where  μ⁢(r0)≡AB≡(C+C¯)⁢π⁢r02⁢ρfm+c0⁢π⁢r02⁢ρf.  In these expressions, P is the incident fluid pressure, ρf is the fluid mass density, c0 is the fluid speed of sound, C and C¯ are area coefficients for the cross-section, r0 is the (equivalent) circular radius of the cross-section, and m is the mass of the beam structure. For a beam with a circular cross-section C≡C¯≡0.  The loading of immersed beams in Abaqus is achieved using incident wave loading. In this test an unconnected array of eight beam elements is subjected to a plane wave incident at 90° from the plane of the array. The amplitude of the wave is linearly increasing, providing a uniform pressure gradient. The beams have identical structural properties, but their wetted cross-sections vary from 0.3 m to 35.0 m. Consequently, the loads generated on the elements due to the incident wave will vary, and the responses will vary due to the different entrained fluid masses. Both Abaqus/Standard and Abaqus/Explicit are tested.
