The length of the plate is varied from 2.0 to 10.0 to study aspect ratio effects. The material is linear elastic with a Young's modulus of 1 × 107 and a Poisson's ratio of 0.25. Nodes along edge A⁢D are clamped. The plate is twisted by applying a force of −1.0 at node B, and an equal and opposite force at node C. Gauss integration is used for the shell cross-section for the S9R5 element.  Results and discussion The vertical displacement at node C is found as a function of the plate's aspect ratio. Since the plate's width is 1.0, the aspect ratio is equal to the length, L. [Figure 2](simabmk-c-twistedribbon.md#simabmk-c-twistedribbon-t-exafiguresect1__simabmk-c-bmktwistrib-results) plots vertical displacement at C versus the length of the plate.
