Material: Young's modulus = 69, Poisson's ratio = 0.3.  Boundary conditions: Simply supported along all edges (Ux=Uy=Uz=0).  Loading: Evenly distributed follower pressure load normal to shell surface. pm⁢a⁢x = 0.1.  Reference solution This is a test recommended by the National Agency for Finite Element Methods and Standards (U.K.): Test 3DNLG-7 from NAFEMS Publication R0024 “A Review of Benchmark Problems for Geometric Non-linear Behaviour of 3D Beams and Shells (SUMMARY).”  The published results of this problem were obtained with Abaqus. Thus, a comparison of Abaqus and NAFEMS results is not an independent verification of Abaqus. The NAFEMS study includes results from other sources for comparison that may provide a basis for verification of this problem.
