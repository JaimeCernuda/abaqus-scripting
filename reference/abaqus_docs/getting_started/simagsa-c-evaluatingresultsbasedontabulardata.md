Check that the sum of the reaction forces and reaction moments with the corresponding applied loads is zero. The nonzero reaction force in the 3-direction equilibrates the vertical force of the pressure load (20 kPa × 1.0 m × 0.4 m). In addition to the reaction forces, the pressure load causes self-equilibrating reaction moments at the constrained rotational degrees of freedom.  This example was run as a linear analysis, in which it is assumed that the nodal displacements are small relative to the characteristic structural dimensions. The midspan deflection across the plate, as indicated in the table of displacements (not shown here), is approximately 5.4 cm, or roughly 5% of the plate's length. However, it is questionable whether the displacements are sufficiently small for a linear analysis to provide accurate results. Nonlinear effects in the structure may be important, so we need to run a nonlinear analysis to further investigate this example. Geometrically nonlinear analyses are discussed in [Nonlinearity](simagsa-m-Intrononlin-sb.md). |
