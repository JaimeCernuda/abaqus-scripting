This page discusses:   * About the Model * Procedure Summary | | |   About the Model The model of the hook is stressed by the area forces of a downward load; in addition, an area load is applied in a second load case in X direction, which illustrates a load case of misuse. The crane hook is being supported by the upper bore hole. To keep the model simple, no other contacts or swinging motions in the bore hole are taken into account.  The surface elements in the area of the tip of the crane hook are defined as being FROZEN (gray areas) for it to remain as such during optimization. The target of the optimization is the minimization of the volume of the crane hook for given constraints of displacements and frequencies.
